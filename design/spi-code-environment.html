What changes

  1. Status line. One line: In use with who and how long, Idle, In maintenance or Usage unknown. One facts line replaces the three tiles.
  2. One timeline, 24 h or 7 days. Activity on top: maintenance as a band, borrows over it, hold lengths printed. In 7 days, forks show a count per day; a count or day heading opens that day.
  3. Lanes carry their rhythm. An empty lane shows its last run, so Reset stays in view. Smoke’s latest run leads the facts line.
  4. Below the timeline. Waiting on you, Stack runs and Stack repository stay. The Version and Fork deploys rows go.

Not measured

  • Borrows are fork deployments to the shared environment. A deployment’s end is not the Restore step.
  • 22 of 77 this week have no recorded end: dashed tick, totals marked ≈, left out of fork overlap.
  • Idle means no fork deploying; a fork can still be proving. Fork overlap is a lower bound. EV-08 lane events replace both.
  • Hold set and clear are matched by time until core sends them.

PRD and design changes

  • PS-09: tiles become the status line and facts line.
  • EnvLane gains borrows and reset; core sends 7 days and each lane’s cadence and last run.
  • Core reads env-reset.yml; until then the Reset lane is hidden.
  • Cadences come from each workflow’s cron, in the user’s time zone.

Decided

  • Sidebar stays green or in maintenance.
  • The range resets to 24 h on return.
  • Reset is weekly: its age turns amber after 7 days.
