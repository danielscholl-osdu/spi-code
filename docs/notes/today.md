# Today as redrawn

What the redrawn Today (epic spicode-ilge) taught that the code and tests don't show. Counts and members are in [today-count.md](today-count.md).

- The flow's connectors (each →, the narrow rail, its turn and the ↓) must be DOM text in aria-hidden elements. The smoke's rail rule (`smoke-flow-rail.ts`) measures elements, and it can't see a pseudo-element, so an arrow drawn in CSS passes no check.
- `.today` reserves its scrollbar gutter (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), so the flow keeps its left edge and width when Yours turns into the taller or shorter Forks sheet. Local macOS uses overlay scrollbars, so a width that passes in a local smoke can still fail on CI's macOS and Windows runners. Trust the CI package jobs for widths.
- The narrow restack is a container query on `.today-table` at 560 px, and it applies to every Yours section (Blocked, Waiting on you, Moving without you, Can wait, and Since you last looked or Last 24 hours), not just the counted ones. The Forks sheet is the exception: it scrolls sideways. The packaged smoke checks the restack only on the busy morning at a 752 px window (a 520 px centre), on Yours and with Sync PR pressed: every collapsed `tr.today-row` outside Forks must have a What cell at least half the centre wide with its Link cell below it, and the one pending request it adds must be a four-cell row of that grid. It does not measure opened bodies or subrows, the calm and bad mornings, or a section the busy morning lacks.

- “Behind upstream” means commits the sync has not handled yet: a commit already evaluated by the filter is not coming, even when no filtered change was pushed to `fork_upstream`.
- Today's lines, rollout tally and in-flight moves come from one projection (`todayProjection` in core), which classifies the rollout once. The host briefing runs it once and passes its `flights` to `todaySummary`. The service home does not get those flights: `serviceHome` calls `todayInFlight` once per build, the same projection over the model and items it is given, so Overview and Moving without you share the rules, not the call.
- A member leaves its counted line only on its own proven re-run or running check. A runs read that fails keeps the earlier read's data, and that kept run still moves the member today (spicode-ilge.10).
- The Forks sheet classifies forks with `sortForks`, and the reachability helper follows it for placement. Classification tests therefore assert through the rendered sheet (`table.test.tsx`), never through the helper.
- Smoke captures of Today states the three mornings don't show (Quiet, Main red, the Integration filter, a pending request beside a Now row, an opened non-rollout group, a red Blocked deadline chip focused by Tab) are variants of the calm and busy snapshots built in `smoke-today-states.ts`. A pending request comes from a `threads.result` fixture, which is a response, not an event.
